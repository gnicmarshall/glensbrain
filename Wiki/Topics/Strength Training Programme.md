# Strength Training Programme

Glen's two-day gym split, restructured on 24 June 2026 in a chat with Claude. The goal he stated: lose fat and be "trim like Brad Pitt in Fight Club", not big. Live training data and health constraints belong to the fitness app; this page records what was discussed and decided.

## Key points
- Glen was training two strength days plus running, at about 95 kg and 6'1" at the time.
- The chat's verdict: the original programme was sound but had "accessory sprawl". It was cut to five hard movements per day, each pushed close to failure, hitting every major muscle about twice a week.
- **Day A (lower bias):** trap-bar deadlift 4×5–6, rear-foot-elevated split squat 3×8–10/side, lat pulldown or assisted chin 4×6–10, incline DB or landmine press 3×8–10, cable lateral raise 3×12–15. Optional bike/rower intervals.
- **Day B (upper bias):** incline DB or machine press 4×6–10, lat pulldown or assisted chin 4×8–12, leg press or hack squat 3×10–12, seated hamstring curl 3×10–12, cable lateral raise 3×12–15. Optional farmer carry or intervals.
- Each exercise has a "machine busy" alternative. Lateral raises kept on both days on purpose: delts and upper back do most of the visual work when lean.
- Logging conventions agreed: bar weight counts in the total (his gym's trap bar is 25 kg); assisted chin logged as "assisted 28 kg × 6"; cable-machine pin numbers aren't kg, so log "pin at X" and beat it next time.
- Last logged trap-bar/barbell deadlift before the chat: 70×6, 70×6, 80×6. Advice after a week off: start at 70, no grinders.

## Glen's view
- "I know the diet thing. Focus on the workout."
- Hates bent-over rows. Any programme must avoid them.
- Preferred Day B when first shown both.
