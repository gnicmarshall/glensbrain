# Agent Stack

**Goal:** Sell a ready-to-duplicate Notion workspace of 15 pre-tuned Claude agents ("credit-smart", tested against Notion's paid AI credits) via Gumroad and the Notion Marketplace.
**Status (2026-09-13):** Planned in April 2026, two blueprint versions written, first custom agent built in Notion. No evidence in the vault of a launch since; the income venture's live product became the [[Freelancer Finance Toolkit]] instead. Treated as paused until Glen says otherwise.

## Key facts
- Pitch: "15 pre-tuned Claude agents for Notion. Credit-tested. Production-ready." Aimed at founders, AI enablement leads, solo consultants and marketers on Notion Business + AI.
- Pricing in the v2 plan: $89 launch (first 30 days), $129 standard, +$40 commercial licence, lifetime updates.
- Trigger for the idea: Notion agents moved to paid credits on 4 May 2026, so every run costs money and buyers want to know which agents are worth it.
- Workspace design: four linked databases — Agent Library, Agent Run Log, Credit Tracker, Prompt Vault.
- Agents grouped by buyer: Founder/Operator (4), Marketer/Creator (4), AI Engineer/Builder (4), Researcher/Learner (3). Five written in full (Weekly Review, Morning Brief, Competitor Intel, Prompt Curator, Reading Synthesiser); the rest from a template.
- Model-selection rule baked into the product: don't use Opus when Haiku will do.
- Seven-day build plan: five days building, one packaging, one launching (Gumroad + Notion Marketplace submission).

## Decisions
- 2026-04-19 — v2 replaced v1: sharper positioning around the paid-credits era, model-selection logic added, sales copy and launch checklist tightened.
